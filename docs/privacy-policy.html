Privacy Policy — Plumber Quest

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Plumber Quest ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Plumber Quest is a single-player offline arcade platformer. The base game works fully offline and has an optional online mode that is OFF by default; while online mode is off, the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect (offline — the default)

None. With online mode off, the app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data, does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and does not transmit anything.

Information shared in optional online mode

If you turn on online mode, the app shares a random pseudonymous player ID and nickname, your online status, the preset callouts you send, and level-clear / 1-UP events with other players, via Supabase (our realtime provider). Supabase processes this plus standard network metadata such as your IP address. We do not collect your email, and there is no free-text chat. You can turn online mode off at any time from the title screen; doing so stops all sharing. Anonymous identifiers are deleted after about 30 days of inactivity.

Data stored on your device

The app may save gameplay-related values (such as settings, mute preference, or progress) locally on your device. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The only third-party service is Supabase, used solely as the realtime backend for the optional online mode; it receives data only while online mode is enabled (see above). With online mode off, we do not share any information with third parties.

Permissions

  • Internet — required by the app's runtime (Capacitor) to load the bundled game content from a local in-app server, and, only when you enable the optional online mode, to connect to Supabase Realtime. With online mode off it sends nothing; the game is fully playable offline.
  • Vibration — used only to provide on-device haptic feedback during gameplay, if supported by your device.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
