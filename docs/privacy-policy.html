Privacy Policy — Plumber Quest

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Plumber Quest ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Plumber Quest is a single-player offline arcade platformer. We have tried to keep this simple because the app is simple: it does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any information. We do not have servers that receive data from the app.

Data stored on your device

The app may save gameplay-related values (such as settings, mute preference, or progress) locally on your device. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Permissions

  • Internet — required by the app's runtime (Capacitor) to load the bundled game content from a local in-app server. It is not used to send your data anywhere; the game is fully playable offline.
  • Vibration — used only to provide on-device haptic feedback during gameplay, if supported by your device.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
